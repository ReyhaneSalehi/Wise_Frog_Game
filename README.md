# 🐸 Wise Frog

**Wise Frog** is a 2D side-scrolling platformer game built with Python and Pygame, developed as a university group project. Inspired by classic titles like Mario, the game blends action, exploration, and light narrative elements in a magical frog-themed world.

---

## 🎮 Game Concept

You play as a human who has been mysteriously transformed into a frog. To regain your human form, you must collect magical herbs and potions scattered across two distinct levels. Along the way, you'll face enemies, traps, and a final boss — all while exploring a whimsical and dangerous world.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🧭 Main Menu with Start, Shop, Guide, and Exit options  
- 🛍️ In-Game Shop to purchase potions and upgrades  
- 📘 Guide Screen with visual instructions and explanations  
- 🐸 Two Unique Levels with different attack mechanics:
  - Level 1: Spit attack
  - Level 2: Shoot projectiles
- 🧪 Collectibles: Potions and herbs to aid your transformation  
- 👾 Enemies and Obstacles including a final boss battle  
- 🎵 Sound Effects and Background Music  
- 🎨 Integrated assets from online sprite sheets and custom-generated visuals
